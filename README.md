# Pre-Token Launch Radar

A radar for protocols that have not issued a token yet. It reads a sealed DefiLlama snapshot, fits a launch multiple from the market's own traded peers, and ranks every pre-token protocol by a comps-fitted implied value derived only from the snapshot's own TVL, growth and age figures, with a concrete farm plan for each.

The board covers the whole watchlist and reaches below it to the early protocols a radar exists to find. Live-token protocols are kept apart and shown at their market cap, never mixed into the ranked claim.
